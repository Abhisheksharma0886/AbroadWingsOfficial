Why Taiwan?

Why Study in Taiwan?

Taiwan offers a unique combination of academic excellence, cultural richness, and technology innovation.

Affordable Education

Taiwan's public universities generally have lower tuition than many Western countries. Costs vary by institution and program. Always confirm current fees directly with the university.

Learn more

Scholarship Opportunities

The Taiwan Government Scholarship and various university-level scholarships support international students. Eligibility and amounts vary — confirm on official portals.

Semiconductor, AI & Tech Ecosystem

Taiwan is home to globally significant companies in semiconductors, electronics, and AI research. Universities have ties with this ecosystem — exposure depends on your field and institution.

Career insights

International Learning Environment

Taiwan welcomes international students from across Asia and beyond. Many universities offer international student offices, English-language resources, and multicultural communities.

Our Services

Admission Services

A structured approach to guiding your university application journey — from initial profile evaluation to pre-departure preparation.

1

Profile Evaluation

We review your academic background, qualifications, and study goals to understand your profile. You are responsible for providing accurate and complete documents.

2

University Shortlisting

We help identify universities and programs that may suit your profile, based on publicly available information. Shortlisting is advisory — it does not guarantee eligibility or admission.

3

Application Document Guidance

We guide you on preparing and organizing your application documents — including SOP, CV, and transcript requirements. Final submission remains your responsibility.

4

Scholarship Guidance

We help you identify relevant scholarship programs and share official resource links. Scholarship eligibility and outcomes are determined by the awarding institution, not AbroadWings.

5

Admission Follow-up

We assist with communication and follow-up during the admission process. Admission decisions are made solely by the universities.

6

Visa & Pre-departure Guidance

We provide general guidance on student visa procedures. Visa decisions are made by the relevant immigration authority. Travel, insurance, and visa fees are your responsibility.
